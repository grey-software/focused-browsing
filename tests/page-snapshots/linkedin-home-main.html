      Friendly reminder to all you founder types jumping on Claude to vibe code your apps: it’s not a bad thing to obtain a deep understanding of your system, but ask yourself if that’s really the best use of your time. More likely, you should be selling. None of this app building is your job. It doesn’t matter if it’s hand-coded or vibe-coded, you have better things to do. Instead of trying to cut engineering costs by doing it yourself, maybe recognize that AI is a potential velocity boost for devs due to parallelism and static task delegation. Essentially a dev can do about 4-8 hours of work in 1 hour now with a good agent. So keep your engineers around and change your strategy to actually leverage all the paradigms we’ve been asking for, like A/B testing, more test coverage, tech debt payoff etc. That work just comes for free now as part of engineering day to day. Choosing to cut engineering to do it yourself just tells people you’re not ready to lead an organization 🤷‍♂️
